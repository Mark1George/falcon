FALCON - CONTRACTING CO. LLC

The Falcon Contracting Co. was established in 2005 and started its humble activities in general maintenance and sub-contracting works in Civil, MEP, and Painting. We sustained steady growth and expansion during the past years which equipped us to enter into the activities of a full-fledged contracting company in the construction industry.

Today, with our team of well-experienced engineers and skilled professionals, Falcon Contracting Co. is qualified to execute turnkey projects approved by government bodies, delivering quality that matches international standards.
