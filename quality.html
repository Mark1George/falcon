Environmental Policy

Falcon Contracting Co. and its employees are committed to protecting the environment through the implementation of environmentally sound and eco-friendly construction practices that minimize adverse environmental impacts.

The company ensures this through active commitment to:

Resource Conservation: Prudent conservation of raw materials, energy, and natural resources across all job sites.
Pollution Prevention: Effective waste management, emissions mitigation, and spill containment protocols.
Continual Improvement: Setting measurable environmental targets, regular reviews, and comprehensive team training.
Supplier & Subcontractor Engagement: Cascading environmental standards and responsibilities across the entire supply chain.
Regulatory Compliance: Strict compliance with relevant environmental legislation and international codes of practice.

Health & Safety Policy

We are dedicated to safeguarding the health and safety of our employees, subcontractors, and the general public. We strictly adhere to safety rules stipulated by Egyptian statutory authorities and our client partners.

Protective Equipment (PPE): All employees are provided certified protective gear, high-visibility uniforms, and hard helmets.
Continuous Safety Drills: Site-wide awareness programs, risk assessments, and emergency preparedness training.
Employee Empowerment: Open culture where every team member is encouraged to report hazards and suggest improvements without hesitation.
Leadership Accountability: Every employee, manager, and supervisor at all levels is held accountable for maintaining maximum safety performance in every phase of operations.
