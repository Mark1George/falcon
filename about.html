About Falcon Contracting

Established in 2005, providing comprehensive engineering, Civil, MEP, painting, and turnkey construction solutions in Egypt.

Home / About Us

Who We Are

The Falcon Contracting Co. was established in 2005 as a professional engineering firm with its registered office in Egypt. We started our activities in general maintenance, and our growth during the past years has been steady and rapid.

This achievement was made possible due to our proper planning, professional expertise, quality of service, and our relentless efforts to adhere to the highest international standards.

Besides being one of the leading sub-contractors in Civil, MEP, and painting works, we undertake all types of building maintenance and turnkey construction. We have had the privilege to associate with many renowned firms in prestigious developments.

With our well-experienced engineers and skilled workforce, Falcon Contracting Co. is fully equipped and approved by government bodies to execute turnkey projects with distinction.

Civil & Structural

Expertise spanning foundations, structural concrete, masonry, and architectural finishes executed with precision and durability.

MEP Engineering

Complete electromechanical systems, plumbing, electrical installations, HVAC, and integrated infrastructure works.

Turnkey Management

End-to-end procurement, site supervision, compliance, and handover meeting governmental standards and specifications.
